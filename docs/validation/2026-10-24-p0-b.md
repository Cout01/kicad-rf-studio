### D-33 diagnosis and the D-45 call

**Cause, part 1: the lumped boxes were built over copper** (the D-45 diagnosis, judge confidence high).
- `place()` built each box from the two pads only. openEMS keeps PEC on every copper edge that a lumped element does not own, and on lpf915 copper shorted cells in every box: P1 2/5, P2 8/12, C1 2/7, L1 2/6, and C2 and C3 5/7 each.
- In verify this turned C1 into 75.5 pF in series with 57.2 pF and 72.4 pF to ground. C2 and C3 each acted as 8.8 pF instead of 3.3 pF.
- Lossless loops rang at 1.005, 1.873 and 2.247 GHz (Q 140–2000) and held B2's P1 at −43 dB.
- Replaying the stop rule gives the stored verdict, so the stop rule is not at fault.
- The same boxes corrupted the reference: B1's six-port is non-reciprocal by 0.159.
- At f0, the as-built chain gives −40.5 dB, verify −40.1 dB, and the intended chain −1.7 dB.

**Truncation is not the cause.** Cutting B2 at the stored point instead of using the whole record moves \|ΔS\| by 0.0008 at f0. Cutting P1 2 ns earlier moves it by 0.048.
