# P3-A: far-field sanity, pass (2026-10-29)

JSON beside this report: `2026-10-29-p3-a.json`. It holds the versions, the references, the calibration, every run's measured values, the PML and speed measurements, the decisions and the machine time.

## 1. Verdict

**Pass.** All seven checks of the Stage 3 design's P3-A row (§5.11) pass on Windows: both dipoles' directivity, the power balance at every recorded frequency, the moved far-field box, no energy growth under PML_8 out to 3 × T_max, the far-field dumps after the stop rule's ABORT, and no setup gate blocking. The engine's far-field path (PML_8, the over-sampled frequency-domain box, the worker's transform mode, the reader and the efficiencies) is fit for plan 2.

Both report-only measurements of design §5.9 call for a cost correction, which plan 2 applies with the cost's cell split: a PML cell costs 6.4 ordinary cells, and a small single-job model runs at 0.38 times the calibrated one-job speed.

## 2. Inputs

- **Command:** `python -X utf8 tools/p3a.py --out <P3A>`, with `CSXCAD_INSTALL_PATH` set to the openEMS folder in the same call. `<P3A>` is a folder under the user's temp folder, outside every work tree, entered through `p0.enter`, so the gate's data folder (its calibration and run folders) sits inside it.
- **Versions:** commit `a39d9b8` on branch `stage3-plan1`, product code `8f24430`; Python 3.14.2; openEMS 0.37.0; CSXCAD 0.7.0; Windows 11 (10.0.26200), 16 CPUs.
- **Calibration** (fresh, in the gate's own data folder): 714 MC/s in aggregate, a pool of 4 jobs × 4 threads; one job alone 589.3 MC/s, four at once 178.5 MC/s each.
- **The dipoles** (`tools/p3a.py`, plan ruling): z-directed PEC wires in vacuum, with no board.
  - Lengths: 12 mm (the short dipole, λ/10 at f0) and 58 mm (the half-wave, 0.474 λ).
  - Feed: a 50 Ω lumped port across a 1 mm gap.
  - Mesh: 0.5 mm cells at the wire, air graded to λ(f2)/20; an air gap of λ/4 at 2.40 GHz (31.23 mm) to the PML's inner edge; PML_8 on all six faces, its 8 lines outside the gap.
  - Band: 2.40–2.50 GHz, f0 2.45 GHz; the pulse over 1.85–3.0 GHz; T_max 30 ns; the Normal preset.
  - Far field: recorded at 2.45, 2.40 and 2.50 GHz with an over-sampling of 4; the full sphere at 5°, the cuts at 1° at f0.
- **References** (`tests/fixtures/p3a/references.json`): a short dipole's D is 1.5 (1.76 dBi) and a half-wave dipole's 1.64 (2.15 dBi) (Balanis, ch. 4); PEC in vacuum radiates every watt its port accepts (η_rad = 1).

## 4. Decisions

- **The PML cell weight** (design §5.9): w = 1 + (s_MUR/s_PML − 1)·N/N_PML, from the half-wave under PML_8 (48.5 MC/s) and its MUR twin on the same mesh and box (226.0 MC/s), both run free over the same 90 ns. N = 326,025 cells and N_PML = 221,778, counted as openEMS's UPML updates them (PML_CELLS + 1 lines per face): 68 % of this small model's nodes. **w = 6.38**, outside 1 ± 0.25, so **plan 2 adds the correction**, counting PML nodes the same way. The plan's probe had estimated about 3.1.
- **The small single-job speed:** the MUR twin's 226.0 MC/s against the calibration's one-job level, 589.3 MC/s: **ratio 0.384**, outside [0.67, 1.5], so **plan 2 adds this correction too**. Without either, the cost under-predicted these runs 12 to 15 times (P3A-2's predicted against wall columns), which the two corrections together (6.38 / 0.384 ≈ 16.6) account for.

## 5. Stated limits and deviations

- **The short dipole is λ/10, not λ/20.** At λ/20 the plan's probe read η_rad 0.971, with P_acc/P_inc near 3e-5: a power balance too ill-conditioned to judge. Sinusoidal-current theory gives 1.775 dBi for the 12 mm dipole at f0, and 2.108 dBi for the 58 mm one (0.474 λ); the references are the L → 0 and λ/2 limits, so the measured offsets (+0.014 and +0.049 dB from finite-length theory) are not pipeline error. The short dipole's margin to its upper limit is 0.071 dB.
- **f1 ≤ f ≤ f2 holds because the box is over-sampled 4 times.** openEMS 0.37.0 accumulates a frequency-domain dump at f2's Nyquist rate by default, so each frequency picks up its alias at 2·f2 − f (the plan's probe, on a 3.0 GHz f2: η_rad 0.952 at 2.9 GHz and 1.35 at 2.99 GHz). With over-sampling 4 it accumulates at the port probes' rate.
- **D is judged at f0, η at every recorded frequency**, and **the moved box on the half-wave only** (−0.023 dB; margin 0.027 dB).
- **"3 × the stop time" is read as 3 × T_max** (90 ns), T_max being Stage 1 §6.4's worst-case stop time, and "no energy growth" as a rise of at most 6 dB, pre-registered well below the growth detector's 20 dB.
- **The MUR twin's energy rose 16.0 dB** over its running minimum within the same 90 ns, at the same λ/4 gap. It is a speed reference here, not a check, but it shows that MUR at λ/4 is no fallback for an antenna: the design's fallback stays MUR at λ/2, untested until plan 4's P3-C needs it.
- **`machine.elapsed_days`** in the JSON counts days since P0's start (`tools/p0.py`'s START, the shared harness), not since this gate.
- **The progress lines in the run's log are in pt-BR**: the gate ran with no gettext variable on a Windows whose display language is Portuguese (Brazil). The report and the JSON are the tool's own English.
