# P3-D: the noise floor, fail on its move criterion, explained (2026-11-01)

JSON beside this report: `2026-11-01-p3-d.json`, a copy of the gate's `p3d.json`. It holds the versions, the criteria, the deviations, the calibration, every pair's deltas, the noise under both statistics, the thresholds, the screen and every step's measurements and machine time.

## 1. Verdict

**Fail on its ±d criterion, explained by the antenna's sensitivity to its own position. The model's noise floor lies far below the design's defaults, so the significance thresholds stay at 0.3 % of f0 and 0.2 dB.**

Gate P3-D (Stage 3 design §5.11; `tests/fixtures/p3d/criteria.json`, committed before any run) verified the 2.4 GHz fixture's pi variant at plan 3's snapped values (L1=0R, C2=0.13pF, C3=1pF) against four changes that move no physics, or only a little:

- **The same verify twice:** bit-identical; every delta is 0.
- **The whole board shifted by one fine cell** (0.3 mm in x and y): every delta under 3e-6 dB and 3.4e-10 of f0, so the model is invariant under a translation of the board against its mesh.
- **The front end** (the antenna, the radio, the parts, their tracks and vias) **moved by +0.3 and −0.3 mm along x** against a fixed ground: the resonance moves −0.53 % and +0.59 %, η_tot moves up to 0.32 dB and η_rad up to 0.08 dB. That is outside the pre-registered 0.2 % and 0.1 dB, so P3-D fails as registered.

The pre-registered split of the ±d deltas into an odd part (the position's slope × d) and an even part (discretization and curvature) says what those moves measured:

- On the resonance, the odd part is −0.561 % and the even part 0.029 %: the slope is 20 times the rest.
- The ±0.3 mm moves measure how much this inverted-F's resonance depends on where it sits near the ground's corner, about 1.9 % per mm. That is the board's physics, which a user moving the antenna would want resolved, not the model's noise.

The thresholds therefore come from the noise proper: the repeat, the shift and the moves' even parts (§4).

## 4. Decisions

Each is a controller recommendation, owner unavailable.

- **The thresholds are 0.3 % of f0 and 0.2 dB, and no BW_LIMIT:** the statistic `proper` (`p3d.NOISE`, a recorded deviation; `0554ef5`), the controller's ruling at checkpoint CP-D on plan 4's open issue 7.
  - Ruling 10 took the largest delta over every pair as the noise. The plan itself foresaw that the ±d moves might measure position rather than noise, and pre-registered their odd and even parts to tell.
  - Here the odd part is 20 times the even. The "max" statistic's 1.8 % (44 MHz at f0) and 1.0 dB would leave compare unable to resolve most real changes: the 0.3 mm move itself among them.
  - The noise proper (0.029 % and 0.029 dB) sets three times itself under the design's defaults, which therefore stand.
- **P3-D's verdict stays "fail" on its ±d criterion, explained:** the moves exceed the 0.2 % and 0.1 dB limits by physics, not by noise. No refinement of the mesh would change that, since the repeat and the shift show no noise and the even parts stay at 0.03 %.
- **P3-E is judged at 0.3 % and 0.2 dB** (its own report).
- **Compare's lines lose "provisional"** (Task 13), with the thresholds unchanged at 0.3 % and 0.2 dB.

## 5. Stated limits and deviations

- **The noise proper rests on one repeat, one shift and two ±d moves.** The even part also holds the second-order position effect (curvature), so it is an upper bound on the discretization noise.
- **The ±d moves are physical changes:** the antenna's open-end leg sits 0.65 mm from the ground zone's corner. The resonance moves about 1.9 % per mm of x there, η_tot up to 1 dB per mm at f_hi, the −10 dB bandwidth about 47 MHz per mm. A placement this close to the ground is that sensitive, and compare resolves it.
- **The whole shift checks translation invariance only.** The model's mesh follows the geometry exactly, so moving the whole board moves nothing.
- **The pool-4 runs ran about 1.46 times faster than the cost model predicts** (§6), the known pool-4 over-prediction that Task 12's refit corrects. The pool-1 verifies ran 1.08 times faster.
- **No loaded step, no orphaned run folder, no deviation of values (`p3d.VALUES`) or mesh (`p3d.REFINE`):** the base's verify dipped to −12.55 dB, so the pre-registered re-search was not needed.
