# P3-E: the ground-pour positive control, pass (2026-11-01)

JSON beside this report: `2026-11-01-p3-e.json`, a copy of the gate's `p3e.json`. It holds the versions, the criteria, the deviations, the calibration, the pair's deltas, the thresholds with their statistic, the judgement and the pour's steps' measurements and machine time.

## 1. Verdict

**Pass.** Gate P3-E (Stage 3 design §5.11; `tests/fixtures/p3d/criteria.json`) put a ground pour inside the 2.4 GHz fixture's pi variant's antenna clearance and compared it with the base, both verified at the same values. The pour moves the resonance by −3.62 % of f0, 12.1 times the 0.3 % threshold that P3-D set, in the expected direction (down). Both dips are conditioned (−12.55 and −6.99 dB).

P3-E passes under either of P3-D's statistics: the "max" statistic's 1.8 % would have resolved it too, at a ratio of 2.0. The model resolves a real change of the board well above its noise.

## 5. Stated limits and deviations

- **The pour detunes the antenna, and its dip is shallow:** −6.99 dB, 0.99 dB under the −6 dB screen. That margin lies within 3 × the depth's largest move delta (1.25 dB, a position effect) but far outside 3 × its noise proper. The comparison stands, and the shift (−88.5 MHz) is far larger than any noise.
- **The bandwidth is not compared** when either side has no −10 dB band inside the simulated band, compare's rule.
- **The thresholds are P3-D's under its controller's statistic (`p3d.NOISE` "proper")** and are recorded in the JSON with it.
- **No loaded step and no orphaned run folder.**
