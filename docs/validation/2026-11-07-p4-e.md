**Widened.** All eight runs are valid and converged, and every pair is present. DIFF_OHM's measured noise, 0.7152 Ω, lies above a third of its 1 Ω design value, so the tool's verdict is "widened".

- **Kept:** IL_DB at 0.05 dB (noise 0.0065 dB) and SKEW_PS at 1.0 ps (noise 0.094 ps).
- **Widened:** DIFF_OHM to 2.2 Ω, three times 0.7152 Ω rounded up to 0.1 Ω, and SE_OHM, its half, to 1.1 Ω.
- **DB:** the tool computes 85.7 dB, three times 28.5503 dB (85.651 dB) rounded up to its 0.05 dB step, from one row: the null's Scd21, which rose from −75.8 dB on the base to −47.3 dB.
  - No Scd21 change from a −76 dB base can reach 85.7 dB, and the other dB rows would resolve only on a change past it, so DB at 85.7 dB would blind all four.
  - Ruling R-13 (§4) keeps DB at 0.5 dB, as its other terms' noise is 0.097 dB.
  - It also gives Scd21 a floor of its own at −37.7 dB, three times its measured noise amplitude.
- **One mesh effect sets every worst term** (§3, §5):
  - The null's split moved one track's bend-corner arc vertices.
  - The mesh lines taken from those vertices made the pair's cross-section asymmetric over its whole coupled run.
  - Without the null, every design value would have been kept.
- **The repeat** was bit-identical.

- **Ruling R-13, DB and Scd21's floor:**
  - **The cause.** DB's one term above a third of 0.5 dB is the null's Scd21. It is a level rising out of the −50 dB floor on a quantity that is zero by symmetry, not a delta on a level the model resolves, and no dB threshold can bound it.

  - **Scd21 takes a floor of its own,** at three times its measured noise amplitude, rounded up to DB's 0.05 dB step: 20·log10(3·10^(−47.28/20)) = −37.74, so −37.7 dB.

  - **Sdd11, S11, NEXT and FEXT keep the design's −50 dB floor.** Every no-op edit moved Sdd11, NEXT and FEXT by under 0.1 dB, and none of them, nor a net's S11, is zero by symmetry.
  - **When it was set.** R-13 was recorded in the ledger before block F1 started, so no P4-F run informed it.
- **Where the values go:**
  - **P4-F judges its positive control at them:** the neck-down's highest TDR level at three times DIFF_OHM, 6.6 Ω, and the plane gap's Scd21 at 0.5 dB above −37.7 dB. Before P4-F's report, a tools change, made subagent-driven, gives p4f R-13's two values.

## 5. Stated limits and deviations

- **The noise rests on one coupon and its victim twin,** as P3-D's rested on one. Every constant's worst term comes from one edit of it, the null, and Scd21's floor comes from that one row.
- **DB's terms:** DB is measured on Sdd11 and Scd21, and on NEXT and FEXT through the victim coupon's one-cell shift alone, with no gap term there: no model gives NEXT's or FEXT's even part. Scd21's gap rows took no term either: the symmetric pair's levels lay under −50 dB on both sides in each gap pair. Sdd11's took none, as no model gives its even part.
- **SE_OHM is scaled:** it is DIFF_OHM/2, unmeasured on a pair.
- **DIFF_OHM and SE_OHM are also the short-feature rule's band.** At 2.2 Ω, levels() reads a TDR level within 2.2 Ω of the target as no feature, where the design's value read 1 Ω; single-ended, the band goes from 0.5 to 1.1 Ω. A feature between the two goes unnamed. The null shows the mesh alone moving a level by up to 0.72 Ω.
- **p4e_gapp's 25 µm edge gap** (C-4, P4-C §5): its coupled run puts each track's outer edge 25 µm from its pads' inner edges. On the d fill fit2d reads that run 0.78 % off cs2d's (95.89 Ω against 95.17 Ω), inside its 3 % gate, and its worst section, a 0.68 mm jog where a lead turns into the coupled run, 2.18 %. Its gap pairs left no visible term from it: the TDR levels' even parts lie within 0.015 and 0.040 Ω of the mesh model's.
- **The product's RAM figure:** E1's largest process peaked at 0.59 GB of working set, 70 to 113 B a cell over models of 5.59 to 9.09 M cells. That sits under the product's provisional 170 (MB_PER_MCELL), as P4-B's to P4-D's readings did. The gate's processes peaked at 2.66 GB of working set together (4.41 GB committed), against cost's estimate of about 6.2 GB for gapm's four jobs. The figure stays an owner item.
- **The schedule:** no block was reaped. E1 started at 16.4 GB free, over its 14 GB floor (R-6a's worst case, 13 h 51 min). Its free RAM was at least 13.8 GB throughout; the floor is a start condition (R-6c).
- **Scd21 near its floor (R-13):**
  - A Scd21 level carries the mesh's asymmetry as an additive term, about −47 dB on this pair. At −37.7 dB that term can move a level by +2.5 or −3.5 dB, and 10 dB above the floor by about +0.9 or −1.0 dB. So two kinds of Scd21 change may still be noise: one of a few dB within about 10 dB of the floor, and one past DB's 0.5 dB within about 20 dB of it.
  - The floor rests on one null edit of one coupon.
  - No null ran on the victim coupon, so NEXT's and FEXT's own floor is unmeasured: an owner item.
- **The mesh's lines from arc vertices (owner item):**
  - The mesh draws lines from the vertices that approximate a bend's round corner, and each line spans the whole domain.
  - The null's edit changes nothing electrical, yet it moved those vertices on one track. The pair's 0.25 mm gap then held three lines, off its centre, instead of two symmetric ones, over the 60 mm coupled run.
  - That alone moved the TDR levels by 0.7 Ω and Scd21 by 28.6 dB.
  - A mesh that kept a pair's cross-section symmetric would lower both DIFF_OHM's and Scd21's noise. The thresholds above hold for the mesh as it is.
