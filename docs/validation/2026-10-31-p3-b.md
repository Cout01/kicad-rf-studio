# P3-B: the pipeline against a hand-built model, pass (2026-10-31)

JSON beside this report: `2026-10-31-p3-b.json`, a copy of the gate's `p3b.json`. It holds the versions, the criteria, the reference's scalars, the calibration, both sides' measurements without their S11, the decisions and the machine time.

## 1. Verdict

**Pass.** Every pre-registered check of gate P3-B (Stage 3 design §5.11; `tests/fixtures/p3b/criteria.json`) passes on the 2.4 GHz fixture `ifa2g4`:

- the resonance of the product's model (`new` and `run`: solid copper, mesh n 2, the Normal preset, PML_8) lies 0.070 % from that of an openEMS model of the same board written by hand;
- its directivity at f0 lies 0.17 dB, and its radiation efficiency at most 0.013 dB, from the hand model's;
- both runs are valid, and the two models' copper agrees within 0.16 mm².

The product's import, mesh, port and far-field path are fit for gates P3-C to P3-E. No diagnostic run was needed.

## 2. Inputs

- **Commands:** each block is one PowerShell call with `CSXCAD_INSTALL_PATH` set to `<openEMS folder>`, one openEMS command at a time. `<GATES>` is a folder in the user's home, outside every work tree and TEMP, entered through `p3.enter`, so the gates' data folder (calibration and run folders) sits inside it.
  - `python -X utf8 tools/p3b.py --out <GATES> --steps geometry` (block B0), `--steps hand` (B1) and `--steps product` (B2), then `python -X utf8 tools/p3b.py --out <GATES>` for the report.
  - Between B1 and B2, the hand run's `hand.json` was committed as `tests/fixtures/p3b/references.json` (`945ec86`), before the product's side ran.
- **Versions:** commit `945ec86` on branch `stage3-plan4`, product code `cab329c` (frozen since plan 4's Task 9 Step 2); Python 3.14.2; openEMS 0.37.0; CSXCAD 0.7.0; Windows 11 (10.0.26200), 16 CPUs.
- **Calibration** (fresh, in the gates' data folder): 873.8 MC/s in aggregate at a pool of 4 jobs × 4 threads; one job alone 601.5 MC/s; four at once 218.4 MC/s each.
- **The fixture** `tests/fixtures/ifa2g4.kicad_pcb`: a synthetic 40 × 30 mm two-layer board with KiCad's `Texas_SWRA117D_2.4GHz_Left` inverted-F at (100, 100), fed from U1.1 by a 1.35 mm and a 0.6 mm track; a 0.71 mm FR-4 core (εr 4.5, tan δ 0.02) and 35 µm copper; the band 2.400–2.4835 GHz, f0 2.44175 GHz.
- **The product's side:** `new --start U1.1 --antenna AE1 --band 2400MHz:2483.5MHz`, then the project `ifa2g4-solid-n2` with `"copper": "solid"` and every other setting `new`'s (mesh n 2 with a 0.3 mm cell, the Normal preset, PML_8 at λ/4 at f_lo, 31.23 mm, T_max 30 ns), run through the product's `run`. The same step is P3-C's ifa2g4 baseline.
- **The hand model** (`tools/p3b_hand.py`, its sha256 in the reference): it imports none of the product, and openEMS only in a process of its own.
  - Copper: transcribed from `tools/make_fixtures.py` and the footprint (AE1's 12 rectangles, within 0.0687 mm² of its 53-point polygon), the pads from pcbnew's `ifa2g4.pads.json`, the F.Cu fill rebuilt by its 1 mm clearance rule, solid 35 µm. The 35 vias and AE1.2's plated hole (the inverted-F's short) are PEC rods.
  - The mask smeared over 0.1 mm (εr 1.23); MUR on all six faces at λ/2 at f_lo (62.456762 mm) from the board.
  - Its own mesh by CSXCAD's smoother: 0.2 mm on the antenna and feed, 2.36 mm in the board, 5 mm in air; 147 × 157 × 51 lines.
  - A 50 Ω lumped port exactly across U1.1–U1.2's gap; a Gaussian pulse over 1.84–2.99 GHz; openEMS's end criterion 1e-5, up to 30 ns; 4 threads; the far field at the product's 11 frequencies, over-sampled 4 times.

## 5. Stated limits and deviations

- **Differences by design** between the two models, each pre-registered (plan 4's rulings and r1): MUR at λ/2 against PML_8 at λ/4; the hand's own mesh against the product's rule; the exact port box against the product's mesh-snapped one; the mask smeared over 0.1 mm; the hand's pulse; its fill rebuilt by its rule rather than KiCad's (0.16 mm² on F.Cu). Together they stay within every limit: 0.07 % in resonance, 0.17 dB in D.
- **The explanation's threshold** is the 1 % shift-equivalent on the hand's S11 (plan 4's ruling 1 as amended at commit): 0.147 on this fixture, against the physics review's estimate of 0.137. It was not needed here.
- **The hand's port voltage over its last 2 ns sits 24 dB under its peak** (decay_db −24.3). The run is valid by openEMS's own end criterion (the field energy down 50 dB) at 7.51 ns, which the pre-registered rule takes first.
- **Both models resonate 2.2 % below f0** (2387–2388 MHz): the fixture's board (40 × 30 mm, a 0.71 mm core) is not TI's. TI's AN043 (SWRA117D) tunes this antenna on its own board and notes that board thickness, a case and a nearby cable move the resonance (5–10 MHz for a cable alone). This is a soft note, not a check.
- **"Nodes"** is the product of the line counts on each side, as `p3.measure` and the hand's line counts give them.
- **The hand's predicted time** is r1's 27-minute estimate to 30 ns; the hand ended by its criterion at 7.5 ns, on 1.18 M nodes rather than r1's 1.27 M.
- **`machine.elapsed_days`** in the JSON counts days since P0's start (`tools/p0.py`'s START, the shared harness), not since this gate.
