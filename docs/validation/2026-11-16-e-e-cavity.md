# E-E: the cavity modes' openEMS confirmation, fail (2026-11-16)

JSON beside this report: `2026-11-16-e-e-cavity.json`, a byte copy of the tool's `ee.json`. It holds:
- the versions, the spec's hash, and the references' file and sha256;
- the run: its job's verdict, t_stop, wall time, cells, timestep, speed and prediction, the twelve peaks of Re Z_in and the machine time;
- the decision: the verdict, the nine matched pairs, the unmatched peaks and modes, and the offset;
- the calibration, with no host key.

## 1. Verdict

**Fail, as the tool decides it.** Design §5.12 and plan 2's Task 13 have the verdict recorded as it came.

- **The matched pairs.** The frozen rule matched 9 of the 12 peaks of Re Z_in, from 1 to 6.5 GHz, to modes of the Δ-buffered eigen-solve.
  - Eight agree within 2 %, from −1.30 to +1.70 %.
  - **(2,1)'s does not:** its peak at 3739.9 MHz lies +2.43 % above the buffered mode, 3651.3 MHz.
- **Unmatched, recorded and not failed:**
  - three peaks, at 2857.3, 5543.1 and 6413.6 MHz;
  - four modes: (1,1), (3,1), (2,2) and (4,1).
- **The unbuffered offset, the measured fringing bias, is −0.99 %.** Over the nine matched pairs, the peaks lie on average 0.99 % below the plain outline's modes.
- **The run is sound.** P1's job converged under the ring rule at 47.03 ns, at a residual of −68.4 dB. That is past T_max (30 ns) and 73 ns under the 120 ns ceiling.

The frozen rule mispaired two of the peaks (§3). That pulls the recorded offset down, but the failing pair is paired right. Pairing by the plain modes' labels instead fails more pairs, not fewer.

A fail here is not a blocker for part A (§5): the bias is named in the cavity's limit line (Task 14), and the solver is unchanged (A11).

### What the frozen rule's pairing did

The peaks lie near the plain outline's modes, not near the buffered ones. So where two modes lie close, a peak's nearest buffered mode can be the next peak's mode:

- **2779.6 MHz took (2,0).** It lies 36.5 MHz from (2,0)'s buffered mode and 62.3 MHz from (1,1)'s, but only 1.8 MHz from (1,1)'s plain mode. (2,0)'s own peak, 2857.3 MHz, sits 1.0 MHz from its plain mode and was left unmatched, and so was (1,1).
- **6227.2 MHz took (3,2).** 6413.6 MHz, 5.0 MHz from (3,2)'s plain mode, was left unmatched, and so was (4,1).
- **5543.1 MHz lost (4,0) to 5700.2 MHz,** which is (4,0)'s own peak. 5543.1 MHz lies 12.1 MHz from (2,2)'s plain mode, and (2,2) was left unmatched.
- **(3,1) and (1,2) made one peak,** 4935.2 MHz, which the rule gave to (1,2). The two modes lie 0.7 % apart.

The two mispairings carry the −1.30 % and −0.85 % errors in the table. Their offsets from their plain modes, −2.75 % and −2.83 %, pull the recorded offset down to −0.99 %.

**The failing pair, (2,1), is paired right.** 3739.9 MHz is the one peak between 2.9 and 4.2 GHz.

## 5. Not a blocker for part A

**The design's rule.** Design §5.12's row for the openEMS confirmation reads, on a fail: "not a blocker for part A: the offset is recorded as the measured fringing bias and named in the cavity's limit line; the solver is unchanged (A11)".

**What follows:**
- **The solver is unchanged.** E-E's CI cases (1) to (7) gate the solver itself. They live in `tests/test_cavity.py` and the review goldens, and pass in the suite: 2612 passed at Task 12.
- **Task 14** appends the recorded offset to I_LIMIT_CAVITY, beside the 2Δ/a estimate: −0.99 %, written as 1.0 %.
- **Task 15's A-76** records the verdict, the worst matched error (+2.43 %, at (2,1)) and the bias. The post-hoc reading in §3 goes beside them, as a soft note.
- **E-E's references, its rule and its tolerance did not change after the run.**

**Owner items** (controller recommendations, owner unavailable):
1. **The limit line's figure.** It is the frozen rule's offset, 1.0 %. Pairing by label gives a mean of 0.1 %, with the 30 mm side's two modes at 1.3 to 1.6 %. The owner may word the line with that spread.
2. **Fringing in the solver.** On this model, Hammerstad's Δ over-corrects. A per-edge extension fitted to openEMS runs at finer z cells would be a later stage's work, if the owner wants it.
3. **cost on PML-heavy specs.** cost read this spec 6.1 times too fast, likely from its PML share. That belongs to a later cost refit, as Stage 4's Task 15 did for SI.
