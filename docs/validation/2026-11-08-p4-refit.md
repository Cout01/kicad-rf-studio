## The cause ruling K-1 names

K-1 names a cause for an SI model's lower speed: openEMS compresses the calibration probe's uniform operator, so the probe runs faster than a model with many distinct cells.
- openEMS's setup job prints each model's count of unique SSE operators ("Unique SSE operators"). The gates' setup logs give it for every row above.
- The counts run from 0.23 % of the cells (p4c_step75 at the d fill, 38468 of 16989210) to 8.41 % (p4c_l90 at λ/40, 141883 of 1686608).
- The ratio does not follow that share from row to row. p4b_ms90-n2-l40, at 4.75 %, reads 0.671, while usb2_hs, at 5.51 %, reads 0.599; the P4-E coupons, at 0.64 % to 0.98 %, read 0.663 to 0.686.
- The calibration probe's own count is not on record: its run left no log in the gates folder.

So the cause K-1 names is unconfirmed.

## The pools

runs.calibrated_mcs gives every pool above one the calibration's aggregate over its pool, 206.9 MC/s, so each row's ratio carries its pool's contention.
- The 25 pool-4 rows read 0.506 to 0.686, median 0.6545. The 7 pool-2 rows read 0.956 to 1.279, median 1.229: two jobs at once run faster than four.
- SI_SPEED, the median of all 32, is 0.663.
- Until a run at its pool refreshes that pool's level, cost prices a pool-2 SI run at 0.663 × 206.9 MC/s a job, about half what the gates' pool-2 runs reached. That errs on the safe side, as it overprices the run, and the first pool-2 run's level replaces it.
- A per-pool SI speed is an owner item.
