## 5. Outcome

**Fail, with no defect reproduced, so part B's no-go.**

The plan's Step 3 rule runs superpowers:systematic-debugging on this record. It looks for a defect that a new test reproduces, either in the pipeline (normalisation, radius, peak or RMS) or in `tools/ea.py`'s specs and code.

- **The shared chain is sound.** The loop runs the same chain as the dipole and reads +0.022 dB at 0.5 GHz, where its closed form is closest to exact. A defect in any shared step would offset the loop alike.
  - The shared chain: the port DFT with its 2·Δt scale, E per mA of the port's current, |E|/R_M at 3 m, and peak phasors.
  - The NF2FF identity holds to 2.6e-7 dB on both cases.
  - The dipole's D_max is 1.503 to 1.509, a short dipole's 1.5.
- **The dipole's spec is the design's.** `tools/ea.py` builds P3-A's wire unchanged: its tips sit on mesh lines at ±6 mm, the port spans FEED_MM = 1 mm, and the witnesses sit one cell into each arm. The closed form uses L = 12 mm, as the design's Pass column writes it.
- **The miss is the frozen form's, not the code's.**
  - η·tan(kL/4)/(2π) is a thin wire's result for an infinitesimal feed gap.
  - P3-A's wire is fed across a 1 mm gap, a twelfth of its length, and the lumped port's current runs uniform across that gap.
  - A gap model, referenced to the same feed current, adds +0.692 dB at 1.0 GHz, falling to +0.674 dB at 2.5 GHz. It keeps the uniform current over the gap and the sinusoidal current on each arm, from g/2 to h.
  - The run sits under that model at every frequency, by a smooth residual:

| f (GHz) | the run (dB) | the gap model (dB) | residual (dB) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0 | 0.571 | 0.692 | −0.121 |
| 1.5 | 0.594 | 0.688 | −0.093 |
| 2.0 | 0.597 | 0.682 | −0.085 |
| 2.5 | 0.608 | 0.674 | −0.067 |

  - The residual fits the gap cell's displacement current, which raises the measured port current a little.
  - The pre-run review had put the gap's added moment at +0.3 to +0.47 dB and expected that displacement current to cancel it. The run shows the cancellation is only about 0.1 dB.
- **No new test can reproduce a defect,** because no code computes anything but what the design fixes. The miss belongs to the design's E-A row, which pairs P3-A's gap-fed wire with the infinitesimal-gap form at ±0.5 dB, frozen before the run.
