# P3-C: convergence and defaults, pass (2026-11-01)

JSON beside this report: `2026-11-01-p3-c.json`, a copy of the gate's `p3c.json`. It holds the versions, the criteria, the calibration, every run's measurements without their S11, the checks, the decisions, the fit2d probes and the machine time.

## 1. Verdict

**Pass: every antenna default stands, and all-sheet copper is not offered.** Gate P3-C (Stage 3 design §5.11; `tests/fixtures/p3c/criteria.json`, committed before any run) ran both fixtures through the product's CLI, one setting changed at a time:

- **Mesh:** n 2 lies within 0.58 % of the finest mesh on the 2.4 GHz fixture and within 0.52 % on the 915 MHz fixture.
- **Air gap:** λ/2 against λ/4 moves the resonance by 0.003 % and D by at most 0.04 dB.
- **Preset:** Strict against Normal moves the resonance by 0.0008 % and η_rad by at most 0.001 dB.
- **PML_8:** run free to max(T_max, 3 × the stop time), it shows no energy rise on either fixture.
- **Stop times:** every compared run converged well before T_max, and none ended flat.
- **Copper:** sheet copper moves the 2.4 GHz fixture's resonance by 0.27 %, inside its 0.5 % limit, but it moves the 915 MHz fixture's response by 0.87 %, outside it, so solid copper stays.

Three controller rulings set how the gate reads the 915 MHz fixture, whose resonance lies above its simulated grid (§4, §5). One recorded deviation lowered the dt floor of that fixture's mesh n 3 and n 4 runs.

## 4. Decisions

Each is a controller recommendation, owner unavailable.

- **Copper: solid stays the antenna default, and sheet is not offered.** `new` keeps writing solid, plan 5's GUI does not list sheet, and the project field and its branch stay.
  - On the 2.4 GHz fixture, sheet passed: 0.27 % in resonance, 0.014 dB in η_rad.
  - On the 915 MHz fixture, the argmin comparison was vacuous (§5). Sheet's in-band S11 is solid's shifted in frequency by 0.867 %: the difference is phase (|S11| changes 0.002 dB, the phase 1.3°), and the fitted shift leaves a residual of 0.0014 against 0.0221 unshifted. That is outside the 0.5 % limit.
  - The same fit on the 2.4 GHz fixture gives 0.31 % against its measured 0.27 %, but it is not clean by the CP3 rule (its residual 0.0152 against a quarter of the unshifted |ΔS11|, 0.0118), so it corroborates the method only loosely; the meander's fit is clean (0.0014 against 0.0055).
  - Sheet copper's error lands on the meander's closely spaced turns, as Stage 1 §5.3's 8.7 % Z0 reading on a narrow-gap line predicts.
- **The CP3 rule** (`b337e4a`: `p3.fit_shift`, `p3.fitted`, `p3.pair`, `p3c._mesh`): a pair whose lowest |S11| sits at its grid's first or last point is judged on f_res by the response shift that best maps one S11 onto the other in band, when that fit is clean (its residual at most a quarter of the unshifted |ΔS11|). Otherwise its f_res check stays vacuous, as pre-registered. The mesh check takes its f values from such fits against the finest mesh.
- **Mesh n 2 stands** (CP5, open issue 5's stop for a "not converging" mesh check).
  - The meander's three meshes span 0.51 % in their fitted responses: n 2 and n 3 sit within 0.04 % of each other, and n 4 sits 0.47 % above them. n 2's error against the finest is half the 1 % limit.
  - n 3 is no closer to n 4 than n 2, so raising n would buy nothing here and would double every antenna run's time.
  - The ratio of 0.093 comes from the staircased 45° track, whose steps change with n, not from an under-resolved mesh. An observed-order extrapolation means nothing on a sequence that does not shrink monotonically. Judged against its finest mesh, the sequence holds.
  - The cell d taken from the feed network alone stands (plan 2's open issue 2).
- **The meander's n 3 and n 4 ran with `gates.dt_floor` 0.6** (a project edit, recorded as a deviation). At the default 0.8, E_DT_FLOOR blocked both: openEMS's timestep was 0.72 and 0.63 of the reference, set by z edges on the staircased track. A smaller timestep changes no physics, only the time. The blocked records are kept in `<GATES>/steps-superseded`.
- **The air gap λ/4, the Normal preset, PML_8, T_max and the flat window** stand by their pre-registered rules.
- **MUR at λ/2 is no fallback for a PML failure.** Its free run rose 21.2 dB within 90 ns, as P3-A's MUR twin rose 16 dB. PML_8 stays, and a PML failure would block for a ruling rather than fall back.

## 5. Stated limits and deviations

- **The 915 MHz fixture is detuned on its synthetic board.**
  - Its lowest |S11| sits at its grid's top end: −1.34 dB at 1120 MHz on a 693–1120 MHz grid. In its band it reads −0.27 to −0.31 dB, and its total efficiency is 3.5–3.8 %. Its resonance lies above 1.12 GHz.
  - Every argmin comparison on it is therefore vacuous: both minima sit at the same grid point.
  - **CP2's ruling** kept the rules: judging its pairs by the S11 curve instead would read noise and truncation on a nearly flat S11 as a frequency shift.
  - **CP3's ruling** added the clean fitted shift, which separates a shift from other differences. Its gap and preset pairs had no clean fit, since their curves differ by 1.6e-4 and 7.5e-6, below any shift. They stayed vacuous on f_res and were judged by D and η_rad.
  - **An owner item:** a 915 MHz fixture tuned into its band would give a resonance at 915 MHz.
- **The CP3 ruling came after the copper comparison was seen.** It moved the decision the conservative way, keeping the solid copper that Stage 1 validated, and it is applied to every pair alike.
- **E_DT_FLOOR on a staircased antenna at mesh n ≥ 3:** a 45° track meshed at n 3 or 4 falls below the default dt floor. This plan's n 3 branch would have raised only fit2d's limit, a finding for any later mesh change.
- **ifa2g4's mesh sequence oscillates:** n 3 sits 1.05 % above n 2, and n 4 sits 0.58 % under n 3. It is judged against the finest, as pre-registered, and the fitted shifts against n 4 are n 3 +0.45 % (clean) and n 2 −0.75 %, not clean (residual 0.034), against n 2's argmin −0.46 %.
- **Ruling 2's fit2d limit of 0.05** on ifa2g4's n 3 and 4 covers a mesh-line conflict: the shared edge cell between AE1's 0.5 mm feed pad and the RF track's 0.6 mm stub reads the stub's Z0 4–5 % high at n 3 and 4 (its probes are in §3). That moves the resonance by at most 0.06 %: plan 4's research de-embedded the first run's input impedance at f_lo, f0 and f_hi through the stub and re-embedded it at the erred Z0, |ΔΓ| 0.007–0.009 and an equivalent resonance shift of −0.01 to +0.06 %.
- **"3 × the stop time"** is the n 2 job's t_stop × 3, or T_max when larger: on both fixtures T_max was larger (30 and 79 ns). The MUR free run went to 3 × T_max (90 ns), P3-A's window, where MUR's growth shows.
- **The copper models were compared at mesh n 2.**
- **Block C4's MUR free run first failed:** drive C: had filled up with a runaway background process's output (626 GB, from a subagent's interactive Python left without a console). The file was deleted and the step ran again in C4b. The other C4 steps were unaffected: Strict, the n 2 model, ran at 75.9 MC/s against n 2's 76.2 MC/s before it.
- **Orphaned run folders**, which no step record names: the failed MUR free attempt and the two blocked meander runs.
- **`machine.elapsed_days`** in the JSON counts days since P0's start, not since this gate.
