## 4. Decisions

- **n:** 2, and p4.N stays 2 (B-6).
  - Each coupon's n 2 to n 3 move, net of fit2d's own, is far inside 0.01: 2.1e-5 for p4b_ms90, 2.7e-5 for p4b_ms100 and 9.8e-6 for p4b_sl90.
  - The plateaus' raw moves (−0.49 %, +1.04 %, +0.75 %) are fit2d's own to within 3e-5.
- **The 45° rule:** undecided, until the owner answers on B3′ (B-4). The design leaves it to this gate: whether a staircased pair stays a warning, is raised to n 3, or is refused.
  - **The levels at n 2:** p4b_ms90_45's level at its diagonal's centre reads 89.10 Ω against p4b_ms90-n2's plateau of 91.18 Ω (−2.27 %), inside 0.05. p4b_ms100_45's reads 105.66 Ω against 100.29 Ω (+5.35 %), a miss.
  - **The whole plateaus:** each twin's, which takes in its two 15 mm straight runs, reads −3.03 % and +3.79 % from its straight twin.
  - **Why:** the mesh staircases each 45° leg, and the product warns W_STAIRCASED twice per twin. At n 2 the staircase moves the diagonal's Zdiff by a few percent, in opposite directions on the two coupons.
  - **B3′:** it would run p4b_ms100_45 at n 3 alone.
    - Its first command is the twin's n 3 `cost` on `p4.project(out, twin, n=3, rule="l40", dielectric="lossless")[1]` (R-6a).
    - It takes about 3.9 h expected and 8.7 h worst.
    - R-6c's floor is 4 × 1.00 GB (B3's peak per job) × 21.7/10.1 + 4 GB = 12.6 GB. It is raised to 14 GB because the block runs over 2 h.
    - It runs only with the owner's go, given at a start of Claude Code with the reaper's pressure kill off or at an hour the owner names.
  - **The outcomes:**
    - **"n3"** if p4b_ms100_45 passes at n 3 while n stays 2.
    - **"refuse"** if it fails at n 3 or is blocked there.
    - **"owner"** if no go comes by P4-F's report, with p4.BENDS_N3 set to "declined" in a tools commit; W_STAIRCASED then stays a warning.
    - Each of the three makes the verdict "fallback".
- **The cut victim:** the refusal is kept (B-5).
  - `new` refuses a victim that leaves the region, and p4b_ms90_cut shows why. Cut by the region's walls and unported, its AUX track rings between them in the lossless coupon.
  - Every job reached T_max: 11.16 ns for P1 and P3 at −42.8 dB, and 10.14 ns for P2 and P4 at −46.5 dB. W_JOB_T_MAX came four times, with W_PASSIVITY (sigma 1.169, recip 0.0185).
  - Its pair's own 4-port block reads Scd21 21.35 dB worse than the terminated victim's.
  - P4-B records the case and does not judge its validity.

These decisions and the deviations in §5 are controller recommendations, made while the owner was unavailable.

## 5. Stated limits and deviations

- **R-8, the stripline's plane pair rang** (B2; fix `75aa0b2`).
  - p4b_sl90 failed validity at n 2 and n 3: every job ended t_max, sigma 1.086 and 1.077, recip 0.104 and 0.099.
  - P2 and P4 stopped at T_max (9.02 ns) at −44 dB. The ring rule carried P1 and P3 to 16–18.5 ns.
  - Its ports rang at discrete frequencies 1.04 GHz apart. That spacing is the F.Cu–In2.Cu plane pair's longitudinal modes for about 69 mm, stitched only at its ends and driven by each launch via. The lossless dielectric left only the edges and the ports to damp them.
  - On those invalid runs, zdiff (1.6e-5), next (−0.06 dB) and fext (−46.6 dB) passed.
  - The fix was a GND stitching grid, 3 mm along the pair and 1.5 mm across it, 1.25 mm clear of every pad and track.
- **R-9, a port's gap lay over its own lead** (B2′; fix `1983391`).
  - With R-8's grid, every job converged at 3.8–4.5 ns. Validity still failed: recip 0.0948 and 0.0895 at 10 GHz, all in S24/S42, growing as f².
