## 1. Verdict

**Fail, on one invalid run: si_pad_coupon_tall-h,** the tall coupon's horizontal port as `new` made it. Its reciprocity reads 0.137 against the product's 0.02, from a current-probe tie in openEMS's lumped port (ruling R-12, §5). That tie touches no decision P4-D makes, though two of them read the run: the tall coupon's port ratio divides by its plateau, 48.14 Ω, and P4D-2 judges its DC point, 4.6e-06. The decisions:
- **The port is kept.** The vertical port's plateau differs from its horizontal twin's by 0.004 % on si_pad_coupon and 0.17 % on si_pad_coupon_tall (limit 1.5 %), and both v runs are valid.
- **The DC point passes** on every lossless project: at most 2.1e-04 from 2P − I, usb2_hs_gap's (limit 0.01). The three USB layouts' rows came with P4-F's runs (R-6d); before them the largest was 2.8e-05.
- **The dielectric stays lossless.** A Debye cell weighs 108.8 plain cells at the calibration's 4 threads a job, as openEMS 0.37 updates Debye materials on one thread. usb2_hs's predicted slowdown is then 32.95, past both 1.5 and D3's window [1.25, 1.75], so D3 did not run.
- **The Debye accuracy misses.** The Debye run's loss over its lossless twin exceeds MLine's dielectric loss by 5.5 % at 1 GHz and 24.3 % at 10 GHz: its worst ratio to the limit is 4.865, at 9.985 GHz. Its DC loss, 0.0012, passes (limit 0.005).

Either Debye reading alone keeps lossless the default. The miss's cause is the owner's, as this plan ruled (§5).

## 5. Stated limits and deviations

- **The plateau tests the vertical port's impedance scale, not its launch.** P4D-1's plateau, the TDR's median from t_r to t_far − t_r, compares each port's impedance scale with its twin's. It does not judge how cleanly a port launches the wave; §3 records each launch.
  - On si_pad_coupon both ports launch alike: the v port's largest |S11| up to 10 GHz is −15.25 dB, and the h port's −14.25 dB.
  - On si_pad_coupon_tall the 1.51 mm v port, about λ/10 at 10 GHz in its core's εr 4.5, reflects about half the power near f_max: −2.84 dB at 9.51 GHz. Its plateau still differs from the h port's by only 0.17 %.
- **R-11: the tall coupon's lost vias** (CP-D1, D1):
  - **The finding:** D1's si_pad_coupon_tall-h read an open circuit. Both jobs ran to t_max (36 ns), its plateau read 1.98e4 Ω, its launch |S11| +0.008 dB and its DC check 1.001.
  - **The cause:** at the d fill this coupon's d is 0.636 mm. Its two GND vias under TP3 and TP4 are solid rods of r 0.15 mm, and the nearest x lines lie 0.1515 mm from their centres.
    - So no mesh node falls inside either rod, and openEMS sets none of their PEC: the setup's PEC dump holds no z edge across the substrate.
    - TP3 and TP4 floated, and the h ports drove into open circuits.
    - At λ/40 the SI rule spans every solid rod, and both rods hold a node at their centres.
  - **The fix,** in the product for every geometry (`8faa7d6`, tests first): a solid rod that holds no mesh node, within its radius inclusive, is snapped to the nearest lines, as the other vias are. No mesh line changes.
  - **Its reach:** an in-memory check of every later gate board at the d fill found no other rod without a node.
    - The fix moved the antenna golden pin alone. Six of Stage 3's seven antenna models hold such a rod at the antenna's d fill (d 0.300 mm): the IFA's plated GND pin on ifa2g4 and its five P3 variants, and one GND via on ifa2g4. The pin was recorded again once, with no openEMS run; only those six models' entries changed.
    - On the gated IFA the effect is bounded. ifa2g4 has GND pours on both faces, and pad 2 joins F.Cu's pour. P3-B's hand model kept every barrel and agreed with the product within 0.070 % in resonance, 0.17 dB in directivity and 0.013 dB in radiation efficiency.
  - **D1b** reran the tall coupon's h and v runs on the fixed code: the vias are snapped curves at x 97.0985 and 142.9015 mm, and its h run reads 48.14 Ω.
  - **An owner item:** on this coupon the d fill is coarser than λ/40: d 0.636 mm against λ/40's 0.409 mm cell. Task 16's SI default, the d fill, is coarser than λ/40 wherever d is, as on thick two-layer boards. Whether that default should cap its cells at λ/40's is the owner's call.
- **R-12: the tall coupon's h run fails reciprocity** (CP-D1 after D1b):
  - **The reading:** both jobs converged (6.26 and 6.08 ns). Sigma 1.0149 and recip 0.137 (W_RECIPROCITY), against the product's flags 1.02 and 0.02. Its v run is valid, with recip 4.3e-7.
  - **The model is mirror-symmetric about x 120 mm:** its x lines to 3e-14 mm, the two ports' boxes and probe nodes, and the two snapped vias.
    - The two jobs mirror each other: each excited port's voltage, and each far port's, agree to 1.2e-7 and 1.9e-7 of peak. Their currents differ by 1.7 % and 2.4 % of peak.
  - **The cause:** openEMS's LumpedPort puts its current probe on the plane at the box's midpoint along the port.
    - P1's box spans two cells along +x, and P2's two along −x. So their midpoints, 98.25 and 141.75 mm, are primary mesh lines.
    - The probe's choice of dual line is then a tie, which resolves to the same absolute side for both ports. The mirrored ports read their current on opposite sides of their gaps' middle node.
    - That node's displacement current differs between those sides, so |S21 − S12| grows as f²: 0.0011 at 1 GHz, 0.010 at 3 GHz and 0.070 at 7.5 GHz.
  - **What it leaves alone:** the line, the vias and the vertical port. The h plateau reads 48.14 Ω, and the v run is valid.
  - **The other gate boards at the d fill:** every horizontal port spans an odd number of cells along its direction, with no tie, except usb2_hs's R1 and R2 (6 cells along x). Every vertical port spans 6 cells, with a tie, but mirrored vertical ports share their direction, so they stay reciprocal: si_line100's recip reads 1.8e-6 (lossless) and 7.7e-7 (Debye), and the tall coupon's v 4.3e-7. The largest vertical-port reading at the d fill, si_pad_coupon-v's 5.1e-3, is valid.
  - **The ruling:** the port outcome is "kept", as the tool decides it. The h run's invalidity is a measurement artifact the product flags (valid False), and it touches nothing P4-D decides. The tool's verdict stays "fail", and the gate went on. No src change and no rerun: a fix changes every tied port's current, and so real runs' S at high frequency.
  - **An owner item,** with ruling R-5's tie-break (the same family): a lumped port whose box midpoint along the port lies on a mesh line reads its current on an arbitrary side of the middle node. A fix puts the probe on the dual line toward the port's signal end, or gives the box an odd cell count.
- **The single product code:** P4D-report-2, after D1b, exited 1. p0 refuses a report whose steps ran on two product codes, and si_pad_coupon's h and v runs held `693592c`.
  - Their models did not change: on `e546920` they build D1's model specs again, with equal hashes. D1c reran them anyway, so that the report reads one code.
  - D1c's results equal D1's: the same job residuals to the last digit.
- **Debye's cost at the calibration's threads:** openEMS 0.37 updates Debye materials on one thread. So the cell weight w, measured at the calibration's 4 threads a job, holds at those threads only.
  - The Debye run's jobs ran at 8.6 and 8.3 MC/s, against the lossless twin's 264.7 MC/s. While they ran, each worker used one core of its four threads: its CPU time over its wall time read 1.06 at 2 h 11 min. The one-thread update set the pace.
  - So w reads 108.8 against runs.DEBYE_WEIGHT's provisional 6.8, from r1's five-pole probe. R-6a's cost, at 6.8, priced the run at 1 h 58 min to T_max; it took 10 h 25 min to its t_stop, 4.58 ns.
  - **Where the Debye cells lie:** the product puts a Debye material on every lossy substrate, so the line's 1.77 M Debye cells, 28 % of its 6.30 M, fill the whole stack.
    - Only the top prepreg's 0.43 M (24 % of them) lie between the line and In1.Cu's plane, where its field runs.
    - The core's 0.92 M and the bottom prepreg's 0.43 M lie under that plane.
    - A Debye material confined to the dielectrics the measured lines' fields reach would cut this line's Debye cells about fourfold. It is an owner item.
  - Task 15's refit takes P4-D's w as runs.DEBYE_WEIGHT. r1's probe, a small model, read 6.8. This report does not explain the gap (the update's memory traffic on a large model is one candidate), so the refitted weight may overprice small models.
- **The Debye DC point:** a Debye run's DC point carries its slowest pole's tail, about 0.002 by a TEM model. The gate judges it by its DC loss alone, and P4D-2 lists the lossless projects only. A Debye run refreshes no calibration level (ruling D-4). Its DC loss, 1 − |S11(0)|² − |S21(0)|², reads 0.00123 against 0.005, and the lossless twin's 0.00004.
- **The Debye accuracy misses, and its cause is the owner's** (this plan's ruling on plan 1's open issue 3):
  - The Debye run's loss over its lossless twin exceeds MLine's at each of §3's frequencies from 0.5 GHz up: by 9.0 % at 0.5 GHz, on 0.013 dB, and from 1 GHz by a share that grows with frequency: 5.5 % at 1 GHz, 9.1 % at 2 GHz, 11.8 % at 3 GHz, 14.5 % at 5 GHz, 19.2 % at 7.5 GHz and 24.3 % at 10 GHz (§3).
  - The fit does not explain it. On the line's εr 4.3 and tan δ 0.02, debye()'s four poles hold ε″ within 2.6 % of Djordjevic-Sarkar's, and ε′ within 0.07 %, at every frequency of §3's table: an in-memory check, with no openEMS. So the excess arises in the run: in openEMS's Debye update, or in the line's field at the d fill.
  - The isolation twin, r2 §3's recipe, would tell those apart. It is not built: lossless stays the default whatever it finds, so it would explain a miss that changes no default. It is an owner item.
- **The product's RAM figure:** D2's largest process peaked at 0.65 GB of working set on its 6.30 M-cell jobs, about 111 B a cell. It sits under the product's provisional 170 (MB_PER_MCELL), as P4-B's and P4-C's readings did. The figure stays an owner item.
- **The schedule:** no block was reaped. Each block started at 16.3 to 16.4 GB free, over its floor (14 GB at most); the floor is a start condition (R-6c).
  - D2 overran R-6a's worst case fourfold, 10.82 h against 2 h 43 min, as cost priced its Debye run at the provisional weight 6.8. It ran on for the pre-registered accuracy reading: a stop inside a run folder is forbidden, and a killed run records nothing.
