## 1. Verdict

**Pass.** Each case moves the row it was built to move past its limit, and every run is valid:
- **The neck-down:** its highest TDR level rose 7.14 Ω against usb2_hs's, past the limit of 6.6 Ω (3 × P4-E's DIFF_OHM, 2.2 Ω), a margin of 1.08×. The neck run's own highest level is marked short, the short-feature rule's true positive.
- **The plane gap:** its worst Scd21 rose 17.67 dB, from −45.56 to −27.89 dB. That is past DB, 0.5 dB, and its higher level stands 9.8 dB above Scd21's floor, −37.7 dB. Both values are ruling R-13's, fixed before F1 (§5).
- **Every run converged and is valid.** usb2_hs and its neck stopped at 3.07 and 3.09 ns. The gap ran on past T_max (9 ns) under the ring rule and stopped at 22.48 ns, under its 36 ns ceiling.

So design §5.11's refine-and-repeat branch is not taken: P4-F finds no real effect that P4-E's thresholds hide.
