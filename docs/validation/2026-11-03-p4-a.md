## 1. Verdict

**Pass.** Every gated row of the Stage 4 design's P4-A row (§5.11) passes: 36 of the tool's 50 rows, the other 14 recorded with no limit. cs2d's coupled pair reads 0.19–0.37 % under Cohn's exact coupled stripline at 0.1 µm copper (limit 0.5 %), at most 1.34 % under Kirschning–Jansen's coupled microstrip, as KiCad 9's calculator computes it, at 1 µm (limit 3 %), and at most 3.93 % under it in Z at 35 µm on 1.0 and 1.5 mm substrates (limit 5 %). ## 4. Decisions

- **"Thin copper" for Cohn is 0.1 µm.** Cohn's form is exact at zero thickness, where cs2d's conductor is a sheet whose edge field its grid under-resolves (about 1.2 % low in Z0, by its own note). At 0.1 µm the strips' thickness is the smaller part of cs2d's error; at 1 µm the rows leave the limit (§5).
- **Kirschning–Jansen at 35 µm is gated in Z on 1.0 and 1.5 mm substrates, where t/h ≤ 0.035; its εeff rows, and the fixtures' 0.2 mm stack at 18 and 35 µm, are recorded.** KiCad's thickness terms drift once the copper is thick next to h (§5), so the design's "≤ 5 % at 35 µm" is judged where they hold.
- **The identities are read at a reference of √(Ze·Zo), and "a step's Γ" as the bounce series of an uncoupled line**, S11 = Γ(1 − x)/(1 − Γ²x). At √(Ze·Zo) a homogeneous pair's FEXT and S11 vanish at every frequency, which is how the design's "FEXT = 0" is read.
- **"Then 50.0 Ω" is read after the line's second round trip, from 4τ + 4·t_r** (1.834 ns) to 20 ns: between 2τ and 4τ the line reads its own Γ³ (§5). The TDR rows pass at |error| < 0.05 Ω, the design's "60.0 then 50.0 Ω" to one decimal.
- **The even/odd 4-port lives in `tools/p4a.py`**, beside Cohn, the other reference model: no product code reads it in plans 1–5, whose results come from the run's S, and plan 4's P4-B imports it there for NEXT's peak.

These decisions, the deviations in §5 and the Stage 1 spec's amendments A-41 to A-47 are controller recommendations, made while the owner was unavailable, as is the vertical port's nearest plane past bare layers (A-44). The Stage 4 exit record carries them as owner items.

## 5. Stated limits and deviations

- **Cohn and cs2d's grid.** At 0.1 µm cs2d reads −0.19 to −0.37 % against Cohn's zero thickness, of which −0.14 to −0.24 % is cs2d's own grid (its reading at 0.01 µm) and the rest the strips' thickness. At 1 µm it reads −0.63 to −1.36 %, outside 0.5 %. A later change to cs2d's GRADE or COARSE moves these rows by tenths of a percent with no bug involved: the worst row's margin is 0.13 %, and such a change is reviewed as a cs2d change, re-measured against the same references, never by moving them.
- **The reference box.** The microstrip pairs sit in cs2d's reference box (REF_H 10, a grounded box 10 h past the strips): at 1 µm it holds about 0.5 % of Zeven's −1.32 % against Kirschning–Jansen, and nearly all of εeven's −0.75 % (−0.79 % and −0.03 % in a box 40 h out); the odd mode does not move. Plan 2's 2D table carries the same bias on Zeven and Zcomm.
- **KiCad's thickness terms.** KiCad's coupled microstrip builds on a zero-thickness single line (`compute_single_line`), so its thickness terms raise Zeven and εodd as the copper thickens, where both must fall. On the fixtures' stack its Zeven reads 96.99, 98.79 and 100.12 Ω at 0, 18 and 35 µm, and its εodd 2.768, 2.799 and 2.820, while cs2d's read 95.84 Ω and 2.746 at 1 µm, 92.72 Ω and 2.586 at 18 µm, and 90.73 Ω and 2.474 at 35 µm. So cs2d reads Zeven and Zodd 9.4 % and 9.3 % under KiCad's at 35 µm, where r1's openEMS probe read cs2d's Zdiff within 1.9 % (116.0 Ω against r1's cs2d 113.8 Ω). The 35 µm limit is met where t/h ≤ 0.035; εeff at 35 µm is recorded, cs2d's εodd 4.3–5.0 % under KiCad's on the gated cases. This is KiCad's coding of the formula, as `tests/test_cs2d.py`'s ruling found for Hammerstad–Jensen and KiCad's CPWG at 35 µm.
- **P4-B against KiCad's calculator.** P4-B's "Zdiff within 5 % of KiCad's calculator" therefore cannot hold on that stack for a correct solver (KiCad 125.6 Ω, cs2d 113.9 Ω, r1's FDTD 116.0 Ω): plan 4 rules on it before P4-B runs.
- **The TDR between the round trips.** Between 2τ and 4τ the TDR reads 50.075 Ω, the line's own Γ³: 50·(1 + Γ³)/(1 − Γ³) with Γ = 1/11.

The readings against Cohn at 0.01 and 1 µm, the 40 h box, KiCad's values at 0 µm and the TDR between 2τ and 4τ are not P4-A rows. The plan's prototypes measured them, and cs2d, `tests/kicad_lines.py` and the TDR at `dd0e345` gave the same in memory for this report: against Cohn −0.141 to −0.236 % at 0.01 µm and −0.627 to −1.360 % at 1 µm; in the 40 h box Zeven −0.789 % and εeven −0.034 %, the odd mode's errors within 0.002 % of the 10 h box's; KiCad's Zeven 96.988 Ω and εodd 2.7684 at 0 µm; the TDR 50.073–50.076 Ω from 2τ + 2·t_r to 4τ − 2·t_r. r1's figures are the Stage 4 research's.
