## 4. Decisions

- **The rule:** the d fill (C-2), and p4.RULE = "dfill" in `a42aa2e`. Every later gate's run is at the d fill, P4-B's B3′ aside: it stays at λ/40, as its twins' runs are P4-B's.
  - Neither λ rule meets ds_max on either pair, and the pre-registered rule then keeps the d fill.
  - λ/80 reads farther from the d fill than λ/40 on both pairs: 0.0181 against 0.0130 on p4b_ms90, and 0.0389 against 0.0326 on p4c_l90. §5 gives the cause.
  - The delays pass at both rules: −0.09 % to −0.24 %, against 0.5 %.
- **n:** 2, and p4.N stays 2. Both step lines' delays pass, so neither refinement ran ("refine" is None).
- **The short-feature rule:** neither step line's lowest or highest level carries a mark ([False, False] on both). Their 36 mm middles are long enough to read, so this is the rule's true negative, and no owner item arises.
- **The product's default:** Task 16 takes the d fill as the SI default. `new --interface` writes max_cell_mm null, and I_SI_DEFAULTS takes the d fill's text.

These decisions and the deviations in §5 are controller recommendations, made while the owner was unavailable.

## 5. Stated limits and deviations

- **Why λ/80 misses by more than λ/40** (CP-C1, read from the runs already made, with no new run):
  - **Where:** the largest |ΔS| lies at the band's top.
    - At λ/40 it is a port's reflection at 10 GHz: S44 on p4b_ms90 and S33 on p4c_l90.
    - At λ/80 it is a transmission entry near 9.8 GHz: S32 at 9.77 GHz on p4b_ms90, and S13 at 9.81 GHz on p4c_l90.
    - Up to 5 GHz the straight pair meets 0.01 at both rules (0.0078 and 0.0099). The L does not (0.0196 and 0.0213).
  - **λ/40 against λ/80:** the thru phase at 10 GHz moves as the Yee estimate predicts, −0.0199 rad against the estimate's −0.0170 on p4b_ms90, and −0.0112 against −0.0114 on p4c_l90.
  - **The plateaus:** p4b_ms90 reads 91.175, 91.175 and 91.176 Ω at λ/40, λ/80 and the d fill. p4c_l90 has none: its window, 2 t_r to t_far − 2 t_r, spans 38 ps, under one rise time (125 ps).
  - **The delay:** the d fill arrives last.
    - By the thru's fitted delay, λ/40 leads it by 0.03 ps and λ/80 by 0.29 ps on p4b_ms90, and by 0.45 and 0.60 ps on p4c_l90.
    - The Yee term alone would put the d fill 0.34 ps ahead of λ/40 on p4b_ms90, and 0.23 ps ahead on p4c_l90.
    - So the d fill carries about 0.37 ps of delay on p4b_ms90, and 0.66 ps on p4c_l90, that neither λ run has. Its far-end TDR edge comes 0.7 and 1.3 ps later.
  - **The launches:** they reflect more at the d fill. |S11| at 5 GHz reads 0.3066 against λ/40's 0.3012 on p4b_ms90, and 0.2722 against 0.2584 on p4c_l90.
  - **The cause:** the evidence points to the fringing at the launches (the pads and the leads' fan-out from their 2 mm pitch) and at p4c_l90's corner.
    - The d fill's cells of d resolve it. Both λ rules grade their cells up there.
    - At λ/40 the Yee lag partly offsets the missing delay, so λ/40 reads closer. At λ/80 the lag shrinks to a quarter and the missing delay remains, so λ/80 cannot meet 0.01 either.
    - λ/40 and λ/80 also differ from each other: by up to 0.0129 on p4b_ms90 (at 9.40 GHz) and 0.0105 on p4c_l90 (at 9.75 GHz), and by at most 0.0025 up to 5 GHz.
  - **No pipeline fault:** every run converged and is valid, the delays agree within 0.25 % and the plateaus within 0.001 Ω.
- **The route-length limit:** 27.9 mm at λ/40 and 134.9 mm at λ/80, at 10 GHz with εeff odd 3.063 and d 0.1 mm. Each is the length at which the Yee phase error alone reaches ds_max against the d fill.
  - The pairs show it is optimistic. λ/80 misses on 40 mm and 60 mm, well under its 134.9 mm, because the launches and the corner add a term that no λ rule removes.
  - The limit is recorded, not applied: a max_cell that follows the route's length is a deferred item of the plan. With the rule at the d fill, no later gate depends on it.
- **The step lines' references sit on the λ/40 mesh:** p4c.references built each line at "l40" before CP-C1, while C3 ran at the d fill. The md's text calls them fit2d "on the run's own mesh".
  - fit2d on the d-fill mesh at n 2, with no openEMS run, moves them by a tenth of the limits at most:
    - p4c_step75: lead 50.823 against 50.747 Ω (+0.15 %), middle 77.261 against 77.214 Ω (+0.06 %), Γ 0.20642 against 0.20684.
    - p4c_step35: lead 50.932 against 50.972 Ω (−0.08 %), middle 35.382 against 35.360 Ω (+0.06 %), Γ −0.18015 against −0.18083.
  - Against the d-fill mesh's fit2d the checks would read: p4c_step75 z_lead +0.65 %, z_mid +0.54 %, gamma −0.0005; p4c_step35 z_lead +0.63 %, z_mid +0.98 %, gamma +0.0016. Every one passes either way, so the committed references stand, with no new reference.
- **The step lines' delay:** p4c_step35's reads +1.77 %, 0.23 % inside 2 %, the gate's closest margin. p4c_step75's reads −0.60 %.
  - Each is half the time between the reflection's half-height edges, so each step's own discontinuity lies inside it.
  - Its edges read 468.5 and 923.3 ps against the predicted 462.6 and 909.5 ps. p4c_step75's read 472.6 and 899.8 ps against 462.6 and 892.4 ps.
- **The level after the middle, z_after, is recorded, not gated.** It reads 52.00 Ω on p4c_step75 and 50.63 Ω on p4c_step35, where the lead after has the lead's width and fit2d's Z0.
  - The localising TDR reads it through both steps. Their multiple reflection adds about Γ³ to its reflection coefficient: +0.0087 on p4c_step75 and −0.0057 on p4c_step35.
  - With each run's own lead level, that predicts 52.05 and 50.67 Ω, about 0.1 % over the readings.
- **C-4, shared mesh lines:** they change no code.
  - Every line of a board shares one set of mesh lines per axis, so edges a few µm apart force lines that close.
  - p4e_gapp's coupled run (s 0.35 mm) puts each track's outer edge 25 µm from its pads' inner edges, and p4e_gapp3's (s 0.317 mm) 41.7 µm.
  - fit2d reads them with no openEMS run. A coupled cut's error is the worse of its two modes':
